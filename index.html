// MORE PARTY GAME IDEAS

  1. Beat Battle — tap along to the beat, best rhythm consistency wins.
  2. Reaction Drop — wait for THE DROP cue, fastest reflex scores. False starts disqualify.
  3. BPM Guesser — match a synthesized click track by tapping. Closest BPM wins.
  4. Name That Drop — DJ plays 3-second snippets, first correct buzz scores.
  5. Crowd Hype Meter — phone mics measure cheering volume.
  6. Genre Roulette — wheel spins a constraint, DJ has to mix to it.
  7. Build-Up Predictor — tap when you think the drop is coming.
  8. Lyric Finish — show the first half of a lyric, complete the next line.
  9. Last Beat Standing — survival round, bad timing eliminates players.
  10. Flashlight Wave — coordinated phone-flashlight wave around the room.
  11. Genre Telephone — each player adds a constraint, DJ must satisfy them all.
  12. Mash-Up Mayhem — DJ overlays two tracks, first to name both wins.
